# FlowForge Component Guidelines

## Hero

Must communicate:

- What FlowForge is
- Why it matters
- What to do next

within the first screen.

---

## Buttons

Clear.

Simple.

High contrast.

Primary CTA should always stand out.

---

## Navigation

Desktop

Minimal.

Mobile

Easy thumb interaction.

---

## Sections

Consistent spacing.

Consistent rhythm.

Avoid repeating identical layouts.

---

## Product Showcase

Product Showcase is not Feature List.

Show products in action.

---

## Case Preview

Every preview should communicate:

- Product
- Business Outcome
- Current Status

within three seconds.

Structure:

Hero KPI

↓

Supporting Information

↓

Recent Activity

---

## Production Reporting

Focus:

- Work Orders
- Progress
- Manufacturing

Visitors immediately understand:

Current production status.

---

## Inventory Management

Focus:

- Inventory
- Locations
- Differences

Visitors immediately understand:

Current inventory condition.

---

## Quality Management

Focus:

- Complaint Lifecycle
- Responsibility
- Current Progress

Visitors immediately understand:

Where the case is currently.

---

## Timeline

Desktop

Alternating layout.

Mobile

Single-column reading flow.

---

## Cards

Cards should support content.

Not become the focus.

---

## Animations

Minimal.

Functional.

Never decorative.

---

## Consistency

Maintain consistency across:

- Border Radius
- Typography
- Colors
- Shadows
- Buttons
- Spacing
- Motion

Every component should feel like it belongs to the same product.
